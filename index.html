FAQ

Good to know before you reserve your place.

Is OneLoveShy a dating site?

No, but trying (and being very disappointed with) a dating site is what sparked the idea.

Use of AI?

Some of this was generated, but mostly typed, from my mind through my fingers. All member images will have a photo date and note of edits. With enough members' preferences, AI will help you find people you want to meet.

How would a business or public figure use it?

Get a clean easy-to-remember place — then promote how you want. Trust-meter rating, because trust is key. Even the super-busy can give a little time for someone who has donated to their favorite charity, filtering serious messages from noise.

What does "not-for-profit" actually mean here?

OneLoveShy isn't built to maximize returns for owners or investors. Message fees and name reservations cover normal expences; anything left over goes back into the platform or community causes, not dividends.

Is the platform free to use?

Browsing is — Joining by reserving your unique place like Your-Name is a one-time fee.

Can I delete my account if I choose to?

Yes, you have control over how you can be found and messaged.

What about an app?

No - You have too many. OneLoveShy uses the message to prevent junk, not to hook you. Members can switch to whatever communication methods they prefer, when they're ready.
