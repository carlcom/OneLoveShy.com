FAQ

Good to know before you reserve a name.

Is OneLoveShy a dating site?

No, but trying (and being very dissappointed with) a dating site is what sparked the idea.

How would a business or public figure use it?

Get a clean public page with easy-to-remember address — then promote how you want. Trust-meter for every member, because trust is key. Even the super-busy can give a little time for someone who has donated to their favorite charity, filtering serious messages from noise.

What does "not-for-profit" actually mean here?

OneLoveShy isn't built to maximize returns for owners or investors. Message fees and name reservations cover costs; anything left over goes back into the community and the mission.

Is the platform free to use?

Browsing is — Joining by reserving your_name is a one time fee, allowing peop a profile and receiving messages is free. Reserving your specific name (your_name.oneloveshy.com) is a one-time purchase, and senders pay a small stamp fee to message you.

Can I delete my account if I choose to?

Yes, you have control over how you can be found and messaged.
