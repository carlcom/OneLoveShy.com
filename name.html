This is a Meeting Place, not a messaging platform. I recently saw a musician performing for the public, encouraging people to scan a QR code. A place like Downtown-Sax is easy to remember.

Your-Name is an example, a unique handle of your choosing, making it easy to remember and find your place. You would be able to put whatever you want to share, as long as it is legal. Offensive or deceitful content will be restricted, but Freedom of Speech will otherwise prevail.

If the visitor is also a member, there will be more shown, like what you have in common, and details that you might not share with everyone.

The ability to send this member a message, and the required donation, is up to the them. No influencer ecosystem here. Senders can rate the reply, even though it might be "no thanks, ..." The minimum cost is $1, but members can set a higher donation, even variable. If any member wants to post their direct contact info, that's OK too. Frauds can lose their place. Member's choice to have adult or offensive material, but searching and matching would need to opt-in to find it, and hopefully it's minimal.

With messages over $1, 80% of overage goes to charities of the member's and/or sender's choice*, with 501c3 documentation for the sender. Besides OneLoveShy.Org, other nonprofits will be options for donation earmarks, like Kiwanis Childrens Fund, Search for Common Ground and the International Peace Institute.

There will be a broad selection of charities.
